Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. Questions: github.com/afoofaa/purge/issues

Short version: Purge works entirely on your device. Your photos never leave your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

What Purge does with your photos

Purge asks for access to your photo library to do its job:

  • Read access. Purge scans thumbnails on-device to find near-duplicate photos, screenshots, and likely blurry shots. Every calculation happens in the app, on your device.
  • Write access. When you choose to delete items, Purge asks iOS to remove them. iOS shows its own confirmation dialog every single time. Nothing is ever deleted without you tapping to confirm it in the system dialog.

Data we collect

None. Purge has no servers, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no crash reporting services, and no third-party code that phones home. Scan results, receipts of what you cleaned, and your Pro entitlement status live only on your device.

Purchases

Purge offers an optional Pro upgrade (monthly, yearly, or one-time lifetime) through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment. Purge never sees your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device with the same Apple ID using the Restore button in Settings.

Tracking

Purge does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not share anything with data brokers. The App Store privacy label lists no data collected.

Children

Purge contains no content or features aimed at children, collects no data from anyone, and is rated 4+.

Changes to this policy

If the policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be summarized in the app's release notes.
